# Scenes and source

- **Matachin:** the Citadel's repurposed spacecraft. [Wolfe](https://reactormag.com/gene-wolfes-the-shadow-of-the-torturer-part-1/).
- **Serpent:** after [Sam Weber's Folio binding](https://www.foliosociety.com/usa/the-book-of-the-new-sun-2-volume), with a cover-detail decal.
- **Shore:** the thorn, sand and discarded boots from *The Citadel of the Autarch*.
- **Combray:** Proust's madeleine, tea and returning memories. [Text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/7178/pg7178-images.html).
- **Casa:** Donoso's orange-tree courtyard, boarded windows and crumbling adobe. [Excerpt](https://lithub.com/the-obscene-bird-of-night/).

Made in Blender. The layouts are interpretations.

Portrait base: Lee Perry-Smith / I-R Entertainment Ltd., [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); scaled, smoothed and recolored. [Model](https://github.com/keijiro/InfiniteScan).
